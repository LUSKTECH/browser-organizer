Browser Organizer setup

Welcome

Browser Organizer groups your open tabs by topic, flags forgotten ones, and tidies bookmarks, using an AI you already have. Nothing changes until you approve it.

Find it in your toolbar

Then click its icon to open the side panel. The shortcut Ctrl+Shift+O analyzes your tabs.

Connect the helper

A small helper program on your computer runs your AI. Browsers can't bundle it, so you register it once.

The helper is installed, but it doesn't trust this copy of the extension yet. Run the command below once; it registers this extension's ID.
